PRICING

$450 per review for businesses and professionals, billed only after Google confirms the removal.

  • Direct: $450 per review, nothing upfront, no retainer, no assessment fee.
  • Agencies: custom partnership pricing tiered by volume, agreed on a 30-minute call.
  • Referrals: anyone can earn 10% of net fees for 12 months.

What does direct removal cost?

Direct clients pay $450 per review that we accept and Google confirms as removed. We tell you within 24 hours whether we take a case, and accepted cases are typically removed within 24 to 48 hours. You are invoiced only after Google confirms the removal.

  • No upfront payment. No deposit, no retainer, no assessment fee.
  • Pay on success. If an accepted review stays up, there is no invoice.

What counts as a billable review?

One accepted review with written text that Google confirms as removed. A star rating with no text cannot be removed, so we do not take those cases and never bill for them. Each review is billed on its own, which is why acting early on a bad review is usually cheaper than waiting for a cluster to build up.

What do agencies pay?

White-label partners are on custom pricing tiered by volume, modeled on a 30-minute call against your retail price and projected throughput. A pilot of 5 to 10 reviews comes before any volume commitment, under a mutual NDA, and we never contact the agency's clients. Read how it works in our white-label primer for ORM agencies.

Can I earn from referring someone?

Yes. The open referral program pays 10% of the net fees we receive from a referred business for 12 months, which is $45 on a standard $450 review.
